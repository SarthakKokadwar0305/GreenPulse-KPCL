# National Compliance: Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) PAT Scheme (Cycle VI)
**Regulatory Body:** Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), Ministry of Power, Govt of India  
**Scheme Name:** Perform, Achieve and Trade (PAT) Scheme  
**Designated Consumer (DC) Category:** Engineering & Heavy Metallurgy Industry  
**Document Ref:** BEE-PAT-CYC6-M&V-2025-26  
**Page:** 1 of 4

## 1. Statutory Background & Mandate
Under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001 (Amended 2022), designated consumers exceeding threshold baseline energy consumption are mandated to achieve specific Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) reduction targets across a 3-year compliance cycle.

## 2. GreenPulse Plant #4 Specific Energy Targets (Page 2)
- **Baseline SEC:** 485.20 kWh per equivalent metric ton of finished machined component.
- **Cycle VI Target SEC:** 452.10 kWh per equivalent metric ton (Mandated 6.82% specific reduction).
- **Compliance Year:** FY 2025-2026.

## 3. Financial Mechanisms & Energy Saving Certificates (ESCerts) (Page 3)
1. **ESCerts Issuance:** Achieving SEC below the designated target earns Energy Saving Certificates (1 ESCert = 1 metric ton of oil equivalent - toe = 11,630 kWh electrical equivalent).
2. **Trading Platform:** ESCerts are actively traded on the Indian Energy Exchange (IEX) and Power Exchange India (PXIL) with historic market values fluctuating between ₹1,800 to ₹3,500 per ESCert.
3. **Non-Compliance Penalties:** Entities failing to meet targets face statutory penalty equal to the shortfall in energy savings multiplied by the metric value of per-toe energy cost (approx ₹10,000 to ₹18,000 per missing toe shortfall).

## 4. Monitoring & Verification (M&V) Protocol (Page 4)
- Verification must be audited by an Accredited Energy Auditor (AEA) certified by BEE.
- All digital power meters at designated boundary gates must be Class 0.2S or 0.5S accuracy with automatic data logging.
- Gate-to-gate boundary energy balance must account for auxiliary transformer losses, diesel generator fuel consumption, and captive renewable generation.
