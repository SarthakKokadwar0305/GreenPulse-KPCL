# Corporate Energy Policy: GreenPulse Decarbonization Roadmap & Net-Zero 2035
**Policy Number:** GP-POL-CORP-SUST-2026-01  
**Target Year:** 2035 Carbon Neutrality  
**Scope:** Scope 1 (Direct Fuel Combustion), Scope 2 (Purchased Electricity), Scope 3 (Supply Chain Logistics)  
**Approved by:** Board of Directors & Sustainability Steering Committee  
**Page:** 1 of 3

---

## 1. Vision and Commitments
GreenPulse AI Manufacturing is committed to accelerating energy transition in precision heavy manufacturing:
- **Phase 1 (2025 - 2027):** 25% Reduction in Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) and 40% Renewable Energy Share.
- **Phase 2 (2028 - 2031):** 70% Renewable Share via Group Captive Wind-Solar Hybrid Open Access.
- **Phase 3 (2032 - 2035):** 100% Net Zero Carbon Operations across all Scope 1 and Scope 2 operations.

## 2. On-Site Solar & Open Access Wheeling (Page 2)
1. **Rooftop Solar PV:** Expand rooftop solar array capacity to 1,200 kWp, feeding directly to plant 415V busbars.
2. **Battery Energy Storage System (BESS):** Commission a 500 kWh / 250 kW Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) BESS to absorb midday solar over-generation and discharge during peak tariff hours (18:00 - 22:00 IST).
3. **Green Energy Open Access Rules:** Leverage Green Energy Open Access (GEOA) regulations for HT consumers with contracted demand ≥ 100 kW to procure off-site green energy at flat ₹4.85/kWh landed tariff.

## 3. Mandatory Machine Procurement Norms (Page 3)
- All new induction motor acquisitions must comply with **IE4 (Super Premium)** or **IE5 (Ultra Premium)** efficiency ratings.
- Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs) with regenerative active front ends (AFE) are mandatory for all cranes, hoists, and stamping presses rated above 30 kW.
- Annual ISO 50001 surveillance audits and public BRSR disclosures are required without exception.
