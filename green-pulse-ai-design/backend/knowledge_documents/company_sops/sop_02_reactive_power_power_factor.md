## 1. Operating Norms & DISCOM Regulations
Under Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission (MERC) and state utility grid tariff regulations:
- Target Plant Operating Power Factor: **0.995 Lagging to Unity (1.000)**.
- Utility Penalty Threshold: Below **0.900 Lagging** attracts progressive penalty surcharges up to 3% of the gross monthly energy bill.
- Utility Incentive: Maintaining monthly average PF above **0.950** yields up to 7% billing rebates.

## 2. Automatic Power Factor Correction (APFC) Panel Guidelines (Page 2)
1. **Daily Inspection:** Shift electrical technicians must verify the 12-stage thyristor-switched APFC capacitor banks at Main Substation Panel MSB-01.
2. **Capacitor Step Verification:** If power factor drops below 0.95 during peak furnace operation, verify that Stage 8 through 11 (50 kVAr detuned reactors each) are actively firing.
3. **Resonance Prevention:** Ensure detuned reactors are set to 7% (189 Hz) tuning frequency to prevent parallel resonance with 5th (250 Hz) and 7th (350 Hz) harmonics generated by CNC VFD inverters.
4. **Leading PF Prohibition:** Never operate plant at leading power factor (< 0.99 Leading). Leading PF causes grid voltage surges and DISCOM penalty. If plant enters leading state during night shift, switch APFC controller from AUTO to MANUAL and step down 100 kVAr immediately.

## 3. Active Harmonic Filter (AHF) Operation (Page 3)
1. Inspect the 300A Active Harmonic Filter installed at CNC Distribution Board DB-04.
2. Total Harmonic Distortion Voltage (THD-V) must remain strictly below 5.0% as per IEEE 519-2022 standards.
3. Total Harmonic Current (THD-I) must not exceed 8.0% at Point of Common Coupling (PCC).
4. In case of AHF internal IGBT overtemperature trip, activate backup passive LC harmonic trap filters within 10 minutes.
