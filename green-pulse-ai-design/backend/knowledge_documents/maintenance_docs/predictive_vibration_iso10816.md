# Maintenance Protocol: Mechanical Vibration Severity & ISO 10816-3 Guidelines
**Document ID:** GP-MAIN-VIB-10816-REV1  
**Category:** Reliability & Condition-Based Maintenance  
**Page:** 1 of 3

---

## 1. Scope and Applicability
Defines vibration threshold velocities and spectrum peak criteria for rotating industrial machinery rated 15 kW to 300 kW mounted on rigid foundations (Class II & Class III Industrial Machinery).

## 2. Vibration Severity Zones (RMS Velocity 10 Hz - 1,000 Hz) (Page 2)
- **Zone A (New / Overhauled Condition):** 0.0 to 1.8 mm/s RMS. Excellent operational condition.
- **Zone B (Acceptable Continuous Operation):** 1.8 to 4.5 mm/s RMS. Normal operation with unrestricted runtime.
- **Zone C (Maintenance Warning):** 4.5 to 7.1 mm/s RMS. Machine condition is unsatisfactory for long-term continuous operation. Maintenance work order must be planned within 7 days.
- **Zone D (Critical Stop):** > 7.1 mm/s RMS. High risk of catastrophic mechanical damage, shaft bending, or stator-rotor rub. Immediate controlled shutdown mandatory.

## 3. High-Frequency Peak Demodulation & Bearing Fault Frequencies (Page 3)
- **BPFO (Ball Pass Frequency Outer Race):** Indicates spalling or pitting on outer bearing ring.
- **BPFI (Ball Pass Frequency Inner Race):** Indicates inner ring fatigue; typically accompanied by 1X motor shaft rotational sidebands.
- **BSF (Ball Spin Frequency):** Indicates roller or ball element flat spots.
- **FTF (Fundamental Train Frequency):** Indicates bearing cage breakage or severe cage looseness.
